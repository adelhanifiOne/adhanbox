═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  Fichier principal  1_habillage-cube-6-faces-A3.pdf  (1 page A3, les 6 faces)
  Variante fournie   1b_habillage-cube-6-faces-A4.pdf (3 pages A4, 2 faces/page)
